About Us

NINI'S KITCHEN AND GRILLS started off as a roadside business, nothing much, just a woman trying to feed her family and earn a living. Maybe the saying is true: from grass to grace! Over the years, our restaurant has turned out to be one of the prestigious restaurants in the town of Ibadan, making food available to everyone at a very affordable price. Kudos to God!

Our Mission

Here at NINI'S, we believe a good meal is for all. Whoever you are, wherever you come from, you will be treated like royalty, with a warm smile and each plate made with love.

Our Vision

To be the home of Nigerian flavour, where everyone, rich or poor, leaves full, happy, and feeling like family.

Meet Our Founder

NINI'S KITCHEN was founded by a passionate food enthusiast who believes that great food wins the heart of everyone. Inspired by her love for food and cooking, she set high standards and made sure she made an impact on everyone around her. Today, her vision continues to inspire everything we do.
